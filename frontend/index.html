🏆 What the Judges Should Understand in 30 Seconds
“LandslideGuard AI is an AI-powered decision-support system that analyzes environmental and terrain factors such as rainfall, slope, soil moisture, elevation, and historical data to estimate landslide susceptibility. It visualizes high-risk areas on an interactive map, monitors how risk increases over time, and generates early-warning alerts to help authorities act earlier.”

Core Mission in One Line:

Our project doesn't wait for a landslide to happen — it uses data and AI to identify areas showing elevated risk and help authorities act proactively.

EARLY WARNING BROADCAST & DISASTER MANAGEMENT ACTIONS

1. Proactive vs. Reactive

Shifts disaster management from post-disaster rescue to timely pre-disaster evacuation and traffic diversion.

2. Continuous Temporal Monitoring

Doesn't give a static snapshot — tracks risk climbing over days (Mon 32% → Fri 88%) as pore water builds up.

3. Explainable AI (XAI)

Judges and authorities can see exactly why an area is high-risk (e.g. 42% rain contribution + 28% soil moisture).

4. Actionable Decision Support

Direct integration with DEOC alerts, official situation reports (SitRep), and community warning sirens.
